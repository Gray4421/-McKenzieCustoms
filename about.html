Who We Are

Built on Craftsmanship and Local Pride

McKenzie Customs started with a simple idea: combine the precision of laser engraving with the flexibility of 3D printing to make one-of-a-kind pieces for the Spartanburg community and beyond.

Every order — whether it's a wedding gift, a business sign, or a one-off prototype — gets the same hands-on attention from design to final finish. We're not a big-box print shop; we're your neighbors, and we treat every project like it matters.
